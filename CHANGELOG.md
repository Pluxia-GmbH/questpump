# Changelog

## [1.2.1] - 2026-11-27

### Changed

- The setup screen asks for the phone app again from your first login. Before, it showed QuestPump on your computer
  alone until that had run once.
- When Send is off on the Feedback page, the page now names the phone app as one more way to send your feedback.

### Fixed

- The figures of the step jacks, the lunge knee drives and the skater hops no longer flick to the other side for one
  frame. QuestPump Figures comes as a new download for it.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added

- A streak goal. Pick how many days your streak counts toward: 7, 14, 30, 50, 100 or 365. The banner at a pump's end
  then shows your streak's day against it, as in "Day 3 of 14". With None the day counts toward the next milestone.
  You find the Streak goal row on the General page, and your choice holds for every character.
- On your computer, a pump's result offers the same goals on a day your streak grows, and the streak page has a row
  for your goal. A goal you pick there reaches the game, and one you pick in the game reaches your computer.

### Changed

- The add-on names World of Warcraft 12.1.7 among its game versions, so the game does not mark it out of date when
  that update comes. QuestPump Voice and QuestPump Figures come as a new download once for it.
- On your computer, a work mode pump opens the moment it is due. It no longer waits until you stop typing.
- On your computer, the voice ends a line where the game ends it. Before, Back and a pump's early end cut every line
  short.

### Fixed

- The close button of the character sheet now works in combat.
- Correct a count no longer marks an old set as a record on a move that is a timed drill now, such as the bird dog.
- A setting you just changed no longer goes back to an older value from another device whose clock runs ahead.
- On your computer, Share opens after a timed pump in which no move counted.
- On your computer, "Leave this pump?" says what is true at that moment. Before, it could say "No move is done yet."
  after a move was saved.
- On your computer, Pump home plays the day's mark and the streak count's pop once, no longer at every visit.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added

- A choice of when pumps ask: "At the turn-in", as before, or "On my next flight". With "On my next flight" each
  quest's pump waits, and your next flight opens them one after another. A pump that has not opened when you land goes
  to your unfinished moves. You find the choice on the last page of Your setup and on the General page.
- Work mode in QuestPump on your computer. You start it and you stop it. While it runs, a short pump opens every 25,
  30, 45 or 60 minutes. Its clock just runs down, and it waits during calls, full screen and games. Bank all puts a
  pump off, and the moves you banked come at the end of a later block. Work mode keeps its own equipment, set for an
  office at the start.
- A Lower back ladder in six steps, from grey to blue: back raises on your knees, lifts on the floor, reverse hypers
  across your bed and back extensions with your heels under the sofa. Some of its moves take a band, a pack or a
  weight. Its group, Lower back, stands under Trunk on the Exercises page.
- A Towel box in your equipment, under At home, off at the start. The moves done with a towel wait for it: the
  sliding curls, the body saw, the lying stretches with a towel round your foot, the towel pull-ups and a few more.
- /qp status says whether the voice pack is installed, as it does for the move figures.

### Changed

- A much smaller download: about 2 MB, down from 238 MB. The voice and the moving figures are two more downloads,
  QuestPump Voice and QuestPump Figures. QuestPump on your computer adds both for you. The add-on plays without them,
  with a picture of each move and no voice.
- Most updates now fetch only the small add-on. The voice and the figures come again only when they change.
- An unfinished move your place cannot do now comes as a move of the same level that your place allows. With none, it
  leaves your lists until your setup lets it in again.
- A move on your unfinished list no longer stays out of your pumps. It counts as one set done on the day you banked
  it, so it comes again when its turn is up.
- Five very easy moves are timed drills now, with no count and no record: the bird dog, the bird dog hold, the one-leg
  reverse hyper, the wood-chopper and the overhead chop. Each runs 30 or 40 seconds.
- The one-arm doorframe rows say which side of the doorway each arm works from. The easiest one is now called the
  Shallow one-arm doorframe row. It was "One-arm doorframe row, feet back".
- A move one step below yours that you never did no longer comes as a new move when you climbed that step on another
  move.
- More to see in a pump. The dim and the pump's card fade in. The banner at a pump's end fills an XP bar and shows its
  rows one by one, with your streak's day among them. The HUD has a thin XP bar.
- On your computer, "How close to the goal pose?" stands over the pose's figure, no longer on a screen of its own.
- On your computer, a pump's result floats in a card over the page and closes by itself. The figure keeps its side
  from Ready to the count, and a new level rises on the XP bar.

### Fixed

- QuestPump on your computer now takes in the settings and the character sheet the game sends while you play. Before,
  a setting you changed in the game reached it only when you reloaded or left the game.
- Feedback you send from the game now arrives. QuestPump on your computer dropped it before.
- On your computer, the game's tile on Pump home keeps its icon when your last moves there are days old.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-07

### Added

- "Don't know the weight" under the weight box when you count a move done with a weight. One click saves your reps
  without a weight.

### Changed

- Quick, brisk and fast walks on the pad run twice as long, 40 to 90 seconds.
- The strict burpee and 12 more moves go at a pace you can follow with good form.
- The lying towel stretches name a towel or a belt in every line. You never needed a strap.
- QuestPump on your computer updates the add-on file by file, so an update fetches only the files that changed.
- A smaller download. Lines with the same words share one voice clip, and every clip is packed smaller with the same
  sound. QuestPump on your computer fetches the move films once, the first time it runs.

### Removed

- The towel rows and the Towel and door box. Sets you did on them count as the rows beside them.

### Fixed

- On your computer, the count's buttons stay in view at any window size, the XP bar after a run of app pumps shows all
  their XP, and move names no longer break at a hyphen.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added

- Every quest you turn in asks for a pump. A pump is a few push-ups, squats, stretches or yoga moves beside your desk.
- A record for every move. Each goal is just past your recent best, and your first set is your first record.
- More than 300 moves, most with no gear, on 22 ladders in 7 colours.
- A character sheet for your body, with levels, five stats, titles and a weekly vault.
- Bank all and Skip, one click each. Pause for as long as you need. Untick any move you don't want.
- QuestPump on your computer adds the add-on to the game and keeps both up to date. It also keeps a copy of your
  progress.
- When a new version of the add-on is out, QuestPump on your computer tells the game. You see one line in chat when you
  log in.
- The setup screen asks only for QuestPump on your computer until the phone app is out.
