# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added

- Every quest you turn in asks for a pump. A pump is a few push-ups, squats, stretches or yoga moves beside your desk.
- A record for every move. Each goal is just past your recent best, and your first set is your first record.
- More than 300 moves, most with no gear, on 22 ladders in 7 colours.
- A character sheet for your body, with levels, five stats, titles and a weekly vault.
- Bank all and Skip, one click each. Pause for as long as you need. Untick any move you don't want.
- QuestPump on your computer adds the add-on to the game and keeps both up to date. It also keeps a copy of your
  progress.
- When a new version of the add-on is out, QuestPump on your computer tells the game. You see one line in chat when you
  log in.
- The setup screen asks only for QuestPump on your computer until the phone app is out.
