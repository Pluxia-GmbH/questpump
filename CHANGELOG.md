# Changelog

## [1.1.0]

### Added

- A choice of when pumps ask: "At the turn-in", as before, or "On my next flight". With "On my next flight" each
  quest's pump waits, and your next flight opens them one after another. A pump that has not opened when you land goes
  to your unfinished moves. You find the choice on the last page of Your setup and on the General page.
- Work mode in QuestPump on your computer. You start it and you stop it. While it runs, a short pump opens every 25,
  30, 45 or 60 minutes. Its clock just runs down, and it waits during calls, full screen and games. Bank all puts a
  pump off. Work mode keeps its own equipment, set for an office at the start.
- /qp status says whether the voice pack is installed, as it does for the move figures.

### Changed

- A much smaller download: about 2 MB, down from 238 MB. The voice and the moving figures are two more downloads,
  QuestPump Voice and QuestPump Figures. QuestPump on your computer adds both for you. The add-on plays without them,
  with a picture of each move and no voice.
- Most updates now fetch only the small add-on. The voice and the figures come again only when they change.
- An unfinished move your place cannot do now comes as a move of the same level that your place allows. With none, it
  leaves your lists until your setup lets it in again.
- On your computer, "How close to the goal pose?" stands over the pose's figure, no longer on a screen of its own.

### Fixed

- QuestPump on your computer now takes in the settings and the character sheet the game sends while you play. Before,
  a setting you changed in the game reached it only when you reloaded or left the game.
- Feedback you send from the game now arrives. QuestPump on your computer dropped it before.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-07

### Added

- "Don't know the weight" under the weight box when you count a move done with a weight. One click saves your reps
  without a weight.

### Changed

- Quick, brisk and fast walks on the pad run twice as long, 40 to 90 seconds.
- The strict burpee and 12 more moves go at a pace you can follow with good form.
- The lying towel stretches name a towel or a belt in every line. You never needed a strap.
- QuestPump on your computer updates the add-on file by file, so an update fetches only the files that changed.
- A smaller download. Lines with the same words share one voice clip, and every clip is packed smaller with the same
  sound. QuestPump on your computer fetches the move films once, the first time it runs.

### Removed

- The towel rows and the Towel and door box. Sets you did on them count as the rows beside them.

### Fixed

- On your computer, the count's buttons stay in view at any window size, the XP bar after a run of app pumps shows all
  their XP, and move names no longer break at a hyphen.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added

- Every quest you turn in asks for a pump. A pump is a few push-ups, squats, stretches or yoga moves beside your desk.
- A record for every move. Each goal is just past your recent best, and your first set is your first record.
- More than 300 moves, most with no gear, on 22 ladders in 7 colours.
- A character sheet for your body, with levels, five stats, titles and a weekly vault.
- Bank all and Skip, one click each. Pause for as long as you need. Untick any move you don't want.
- QuestPump on your computer adds the add-on to the game and keeps both up to date. It also keeps a copy of your
  progress.
- When a new version of the add-on is out, QuestPump on your computer tells the game. You see one line in chat when you
  log in.
- The setup screen asks only for QuestPump on your computer until the phone app is out.
