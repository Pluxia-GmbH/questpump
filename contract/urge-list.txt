# What no coach line says beyond the health-claim deny-list (docs/plan.md slice 9): urging past a safe limit (Apple App
# Store Review Guideline 1.4.5: apps "should not urge customers to participate in activities ... in a way that risks
# physical harm"), and the body's measures the coach never names (calories, fat, the heart), which the addon itself may
# say ("heart rate") and so stay out of the shared list. The matching rule is deny-list.txt's: one stem a line, '#'
# starts a comment, a stem matches a text when it starts a word there, case-insensitively, and the words of a stem of
# several words stand in a row with spaces or hyphens between them, typographic spaces, hyphens and apostrophes read as
# ASCII ones. The phone apps refuse a talk or a Chronicle with a match here or in deny-list.txt, and nothing is said
# instead. The game's own words stay the game's: an "all-out window" is a move the addon names, never a line the coach
# says, and two stop lines in contract/moves.json name holding the breath as a stop, which the coach's input no longer
# carries since 2026-10-09 (it gets no cue or stop line).
